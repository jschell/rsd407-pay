# Plan 06 — Enrollment and inflation normalization

## Goal

Put staffing/pay changes in context.

## Work

- Add authoritative annual RSD407 enrollment.
- Compute employees and FTE per 1,000 students.
- Add CPI series from an authoritative source.
- Choose and document a constant-dollar base year.
- Produce nominal and real compensation trends.
- Keep CPI methodology independent from OSPI parsing.

## Acceptance

Every normalized metric states its denominator/base year and can be reproduced from cited source series.
